The Search & Vision

I’m a 19-year-old Computer Technology student at YCCE, and honestly, I’m still figuring myself out. I want to become better—not just academically, but as a person. My goal is to become confident, disciplined, independent, and someone my future self can be genuinely proud of.

Honest Self-Reflection

I’m naturally quiet and introverted. Sometimes I struggle to talk to people or express myself properly. I overthink things, especially about how people see me, and sometimes compare myself with others. But I know I’m trying every single day.

Learning Curiosity

Sometimes I understand new concepts quickly, and sometimes even a simple concept feels difficult. But I don't give up—I keep asking questions until I truly understand it. Curiosity and perseverance keep me moving forward.
